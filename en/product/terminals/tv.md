**STM Link Rooms** is the large-display edition of our meeting product. Install it on a meeting room's Android TV or large display, and use a **remote control** to enter meetings, run meetings, and cast,
all in the same meeting as people on phones and computers.

![STM Link Rooms home](/zh/product/terminals/images/tv-home.jpg)

---

## Designed for meeting rooms

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Remote-control operation** | Every screen supports remote-control focus navigation, with a built-in large-screen number pad and full keyboard |
| **Meeting-room account** | Each display is bound to one meeting-room account and is online as soon as it powers on, without relying on anyone's phone |
| **Three entry points** | The home page has just three large cards—"Join meeting", "Instant meeting", and "Wireless casting"—visible at a glance |
| **Fixed landscape** | Laid out for 16:9 large displays, readable from a distance |

---

## Main features

**Join meeting**: enter the meeting number; before entering, you can turn the microphone, camera, and speaker on or off. If the meeting has a password, you're prompted for it.

![Join meeting](/zh/product/terminals/images/tv-join.jpg)

**Instant meeting**: enter a meeting name to start right away; during the meeting you can show the meeting number and QR code so others can enter.

**Wireless casting**: the **cast code** is always shown on the home page and top bar. Members open STM Link on their phones, tap "Wireless casting", and enter the cast code to cast the phone screen to the large display.

**During the meeting**:

![In-meeting view](/zh/product/terminals/images/tv-meeting.jpg)

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Audio and video | Microphone, speaker, and camera on/off |
| Layout | Grid (with paging), speaker, and thumbnail views |
| Invite | Show the meeting number, password, and QR code for entering |
| Member management (host) | Rename, mute / unmute, video on/off, stop sharing, remove from the meeting, mute all |
| Whiteboard | Start whiteboard collaboration during the meeting |
| Cloud recording | Start / stop recording with one click |
| Quality check | View bitrate, frame rate, packet loss, and latency for network, audio, video, and sharing |

**Settings**: microphone / camera / speaker on by default when entering, voice activation, camera, microphone, and speaker selection, and update checks.
